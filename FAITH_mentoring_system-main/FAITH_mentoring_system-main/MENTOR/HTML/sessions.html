Accepted Files and iPhone Guidelines

Please upload a clear photograph of the mentoring session or a PDF document. Accepted formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, and PDF, with a maximum file size of 20 MB.

HEIC/HEIF and RAW image formats are not supported. Please export a JPG or PNG copy, or use the Take Photo option below. iPhone users may disable RAW capture under Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible. Files exceeding 20 MB should be resized or compressed while preserving image clarity.
